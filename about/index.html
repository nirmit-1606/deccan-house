Deccan House About Us

A Slice of Hyderabad in Toronto!

Step into a world where tradition meets flavor, and every bite tells a story. At Deccan House, we bring the rich culinary heritage of Hyderabad straight to your plate. From the smoky aromas of the famous Dum Biryani to the irresistible sweetness of Qubani ka Meetha, our menu celebrates the timeless recipes that have been passed down through generations.

Deccan House brings the rich, royal flavours of Hyderabad to Toronto — from slow-cooked dum biryanis to hand-pressed kebabs and homestyle desserts.
